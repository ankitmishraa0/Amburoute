1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

In acute medical emergencies (STEMI cardiac arrest, severe poly-trauma), survival depends upon the "Golden Hour". In standard city environments, ambulances suffer from three catastrophic bottlenecks:

  • Traffic Signal Stalls: Ambulances lose 12 to 20 minutes trapped at urban intersections with zero automated preemption.
  • Blind Hospital Transfer: Ambulances often transport patients to the closest hospital only to find zero ICU beds or no Cath Lab, requiring a fatal second transit.
  • Unprepared Emergency Teams: Emergency doctors receive no pre-arrival telemetry and must scramble for ventilators after arrival.

AmbuRoute's Solution: A unified digital emergency infrastructure that preempts traffic lights 500m ahead, scores regional hospitals using multi-criteria algorithms, and beams real-time vitals & ECG waveforms to doctors before arrival.

3. Mathematical & Algorithmic Formulations

A. Haversine Great-Circle Geodesic Distance

Used to calculate accurate surface distance between coordinates on Earth:

a = sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1) · cos(lat2) · sin²(Δlng/2)
c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1−a))
Distance (km) = 6371 · c

B. Multi-Criteria Decision Matrix (MCDM) Equation

Computes composite hospital suitability score (0-100):

Composite_Score = (0.35 × Travel_Score) + (0.30 × Trauma_Score) + (0.20 × Bed_Score) + (0.15 × Queue_Score)

4. Viva Voce Master Q&A (Top 10 High-Frequency Questions)

Q1: How is AmbuRoute different from Google Maps?
Google Maps only optimizes transit for generic vehicles. It cannot trigger V2I traffic light green corridors, has no visibility into hospital ICU bed vacancies, and cannot stream live ECG waveforms to emergency doctors. AmbuRoute is an integrated Emergency Response OS.
Q2: Why WebSockets instead of REST APIs?
REST polling introduces 500-2000ms latency and high HTTP header overhead. WebSockets maintain a full-duplex TCP stream with under 15ms latency, critical for 60 FPS ambulance tracking and instant traffic light preemption.
Q3: Why use Random Forest for clinical triage?
Random Forest captures non-linear interactions across vital signs (e.g. low systolic BP + high heart rate = cardiogenic shock) and avoids overfitting on tabular medical telemetry compared to single decision trees or complex black-box neural nets.
Q4: How does V2I hardware connect in the real world?
In smart cities, intersection traffic controllers (Siemens, Philips, Indian ITMS) support NTCIP 1202 or Opticom protocols. When the ambulance crosses the 500m geofence, AmbuRoute fires an authenticated MQTT/REST webhook to the ITMS, granting a green priority wave.
Q5: Explain how dynamic traffic jam detour works.
When sudden road congestion is injected, the engine detects the obstruction on the primary road segment and instantly computes a +0.0035 lat arterial bypass, updating waypoints, ETA, and signal sequences in under 1 second.
Q6: Why is the UI designed in Red & White?
Red & White is the globally recognized identity of emergency medical services (108 Ambulance, Red Cross, Swiss EMS, and Zomato consumer clarity). It provides visual urgency with clean clinical legibility.

6. 100% Free Cloud Deployment Guide

A. Backend Deployment (Render.com - Free Python Hosting)

  1. Sign up at https://render.com with GitHub.
  2. Click New + -> Web Service and select your repository.
  3. Set Root Directory: backend
  4. Set Build Command: pip install -r requirements.txt
  5. Set Start Command: uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port $PORT
  6. Click Deploy Web Service. You will get a free live URL (e.g. https://amburoute-api.onrender.com).

B. Frontend Deployment (Vercel.com - Free React Hosting)

  1. Sign up at https://vercel.com with GitHub.
  2. Click Add New Project and import amburoute-ai.
  3. Set Root Directory: frontend
  4. Framework Preset: Vite (Build: npm run build, Output: dist).
  5. In Environment Variables, add: VITE_API_URL = https://amburoute-api.onrender.com
  6. Click Deploy. In 40 seconds, your site is live worldwide on a free .vercel.app domain!
