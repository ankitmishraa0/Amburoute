1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

English: In metropolitan cities, emergency ambulances face average transit delays of 15 to 30 minutes due to traffic bottlenecks, unsynchronized intersections, and blind hospital handoffs. AmbuRoute bridges this gap by creating an IoT-driven communication mesh connecting Ambulance Crew, City Traffic Police (ITMS), and Hospital Emergency Departments.

Hinglish Explanation: Shahar me ambulance traffic jam aur red light par phas jaati hai, aur hospital pahunchne par pata chalta hai ki ICU bed khali nahi hai. AmbuRoute is problem ko solve karta hai: Ye raste ke signals ko advance me Green karta hai aur hospital ER ko live vitals bhej kar pehle se bed book karwa deta hai.

4. Master Viva Voce Q&A Handbook (English + Hinglish)

Q1: How does the V2I Traffic Preemption work technically?

Technical Answer: The ambulance broadcasts its GPS latitude, longitude, and heading at 10 Hz over 5G C-V2X. When entering the 500m geofence buffer of an intersection, the V2I controller calculates ETA and initiates cross-traffic yellow clearance, holding green priority until the ambulance clears the node.

Hinglish: Ambulance jab kisi signal se 500m door hoti hai, to system automated handshake bhejta hai. Signal cross-traffic ko yellow se red kar deta hai aur ambulance ke aate hi green wave activate kar deta hai.

Q2: Why did you use WebSockets instead of REST API polling?

Technical Answer: HTTP REST polling introduces HTTP header overhead and 1-2 second latency delays. WebSockets maintain a persistent full-duplex TCP socket that streams telemetry at under 15ms latency, crucial for live ECG and GPS coordinates.

Hinglish: REST API me baar-baar request bhejni padti hai jisse delay hota hai. WebSocket me ek baar connection ban kar live data lagatar flow hota rehta hai bina kisi latency ke.

Q3: What is the benefit of the Super Administrator Deck?

Technical Answer: It allows full administrative root authority to manipulate vehicle speed, inject patient vitals (Heart rate, BP, SpO2), trigger full-corridor green waves, or modify hospital bed availability in real-time during live demonstrations.

Hinglish: Examiner ke samne live demo dene ke liye Administrator panel banaya gaya hai, jisse hum bina code change kiye browser se hi speed, vitals, signals aur hospital beds ko instantly change kar sakte hain.

Q4: How does the algorithm handle edge cases like traffic jams?

Technical Answer: The routing engine dynamically computes alternative routes based on road impedance and congestion metrics. If a corridor is gridlocked, the system calculates a reroute and shifts signal preemption to the new intersection sequence.

Hinglish: Agar kisi road par traffic jam inject ho jata hai, to system route re-calculate karta hai aur naye raste ke signals ko green wave me convert kar deta hai.
